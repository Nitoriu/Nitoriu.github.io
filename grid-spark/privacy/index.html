Grid Spark: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short: Grid Spark does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Who we are

Grid Spark is a mobile game made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Grid Spark on Android.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it does not send anything about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a few files in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your settings (for example sound and vibration on or off);
  • your game progress (for example heroes you have unlocked).

These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used only for optional haptic feedback during play. It is off by default.

The game does not request access to the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Grid Spark shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share any data.

Google Play

You download the game from Google Play. Google may collect information such as crash reports and app performance data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). If you allow Android to share crash reports, we may see anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics in the Google Play Console. They do not identify you.

Purchases

The current version of the game has no in-app purchases. If that changes, payments will be handled only by Google Play, and we will never see your payment details. This policy will be updated before such a change.

Children

Grid Spark is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version of the game is released.
